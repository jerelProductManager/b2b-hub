The B2B Measurement Stack

Four offerings, four owners, one metric tree — ordered by the lifecycle it describes: get found, get understood, get an account, get them back.

Illustrative figures. No data source is connected. Every number is a plausible placeholder showing the shape of a metric, never a B&H reading. Anything marked not instrumented shows an em dash because it cannot be measured at any value today.

Four offerings, four different conversion objects. An approved org account, a verified student, and two lead forms are not the same event and should not be added together. What does roll up is the rate: Qualified Action Rate — the property's own conversion divided by its qualified sessions. Different numerators, comparable denominators, one number Manny can read across all four without anyone having to pretend a Studio inquiry equals a signed B2B account.
And two of the four are the same shape. The Studio and School Safety both convert on a lead form. They should share one event schema and one lead-to-account bridge rather than inventing two — which is worth settling now, while School Safety is still in development and The Studio is still in design.

EDU Advantage partial

Search visibility and on-page behaviour are readable. The verification funnel is not: students leave for UNiDAYS and come back counted as new referral traffic, so the middle of the funnel is missing. There is also no account layer to reactivate — students re-verify annually rather than returning to a portal.

The Studio no data

Nothing to chart. The page carries no GA4 tag, so no traffic, engagement or inquiry data has ever been collected. Tagging it is a standalone task with no dependency on the CMS build or the redesign — see gap 03.

School Safety pre-launch

Nothing to chart until the page ships. Unlike the other three, this one can be instrumented correctly on day one — the event schema is on the School Safety launch tab.

Do the buyers who can actually transact know these pages exist? Awareness here is qualified reach, not reach. A procurement officer searching a cooperative contract by name is worth more than a thousand generic sessions, and the metrics are chosen to say so.

The Studio — a canonical tag pointing at the wrong page action needed

The CMS sets The Studio's canonical URL to /find/b2b/studio.jsp, but the live page the hub navigation links to is /find/shared/b2bHub/theStudio.jsp. If that canonical ships as configured it tells search engines to credit a different URL — on a page that already has no analytics to notice the damage. Worth checking before the redesign, not after.

School Safety — the keyword set is aiming at content that doesn't exist yet decision

The SEO strategy targets federal grant spending, but the design contains no grant module and no downloadable guide — Alyssa's Law is the only regulatory content present. Either build the grant section or drop grant terms from AW-02, because right now the metric would be scoring us against a page we never made.

03 — Acquisition

Did the visit produce something? What "something" means differs by offering — an approved account, a verified student, an inquiry — so this pillar keeps four separate funnels and compares them only on rate. Note that the hub funnel does not end at "approved": an approved account that never orders is a support cost, not a win.

AC-01Hub → sign-up start not instrumented—No CTA exists to click
AC-03Submit → approved partial62%Backend only, no funnel join
AC-05Median time to first order partial23dApproved → first transaction
AC-10Lead form completion not instrumented—Studio and Safety both
Why AC-01 is blank rather than low. The hub home page has no sign-up call to action anywhere on it — the only way forward is a segment tile, and Sign Up first appears one level deeper. The top of the funnel isn't underperforming; it doesn't exist. That's a product fix before it's a measurement fix.

The Studio — an inquiry form nobody can count no data

The conversion path is a "Contact Us Online" anchor to a form asking for Email, Name, Phone and a message. There is no submit event, no field tracking, and no way to attribute an inquiry to the traffic that produced it. Inquiries arrive in an inbox and the funnel that produced them is invisible.

04 — Reactivation

Does the offering earn a second visit? Once a customer has a punchout portal their shopping need is met elsewhere and the Hub's job changes entirely — it becomes the account layer. Every metric here is unmeasurable today for one shared reason: logged-in accounts have no identity in analytics.

RE-01Authenticated return rate not instrumented—No account id in analytics
RE-02365-day dormancy rate partial36%+4.1 pts in 4 quarters
RE-04Contract expiry coverage not instrumented—No expiry notice exists
RE-06Portal utilization partial71%Portals with an order in 90d
Sequence this pillar with Phase 2, not after it. The logged-in dashboard work already commits to shipping every section with a paired metric. RE-04 through RE-08 are those pairings written down in advance — an expiring-contracts module is worth building precisely because RE-04 says nobody is being told, and stops being worth building the moment RE-04 says they are.

EDU Advantage — there is no account to reactivate n/a

Students don't return to a portal; they re-verify when their status lapses. The equivalent metric here is annual re-verification rate and repeat purchase within the discount cohort — a retention question with a different shape, which is why the hub's reactivation metrics shouldn't be copied across.

The Studio — reactivation is a relationship, not a session no data

Studio customers come back for the next installation or the next training, on a cycle measured in quarters. The meaningful metric is repeat engagement per client, which lives in the Studio team's records rather than in analytics — worth defining alongside the redesign.

School Safety — out of scope by design n/a

The page produces leads, and a lead reactivates as a B2B account or not at all. Its retention question is answered by AC-09 on the acquisition tab, not here.

Instrumentation gaps

Eleven findings, ranked by how many metrics each one unblocks. Most came from walking the live pages and reading the Figma rather than from the PRD — they're things anyone can go verify this afternoon.

01

No sign-up CTA on the hub landing page critical B2B Hub

The home page offers only twelve segment tiles. Sign Up first appears one level down, on the segment page. There is no top-of-funnel action to measure, which is why the baseline conversion question has stayed open.

UnblocksAC-01, AC-07, AC-00
FixPRD P0 Hub UX story, plus signup_cta_click carrying cta_location.
02

The sign-up form is a hash-routed SPA critical B2B Hub

Registration lives at /find/shared/b2b/b2bSignUp.jsp/org/HE#/createAcc. Hash changes don't fire page_view in GA4 by default, so no step in the form is observable — arrivals and approvals are visible, with nothing in between.

UnblocksAC-02, UX-04
Fixsignup_step on every hash route change, with step_name and step_index.
03

The Studio carries no GA4 tag at all critical The Studio

Zero traffic or engagement data has ever existed for this page. It needs no redesign, no ticket queue and no Phase 1 dependency — and until it's tagged, the redesign will ship with no before to compare against.

UnblocksAW-07, AC-11, RE-10
FixStandalone tagging task. Ship it before any baseline period starts.
04

The funnel spans two URL namespaces high B2B Hub

The hub sits under /find/shared/b2bHub/ but sign-up sits under /find/shared/b2b/. Any path-scoped report silently cuts the funnel in half at the moment that matters.

UnblocksAC-01 – AC-03
FixDefine each property as a GA4 content group, never a path prefix.
05

Federal routes outside the hub entirely high B2B Hub

Eleven segment tiles go to /organizations.jsp/org/{CODE}. Federal Marketplace goes to /find/gsa.jsp — a different template in a different namespace. One of twelve segments, arguably the most valuable, is absent from any hub-scoped view.

UnblocksAW-05, AC-06
FixAdd /find/gsa.jsp to the hub content group, tagged segment = FED.
06

Segment identity is buried in the URL path high B2B Hub

Segment is a path suffix — /org/HE, /org/SLK, /org/CPP — not a parameter or data-layer value. Without extraction there's no segment dimension, and every segment question becomes a manual URL exercise.

UnblocksAW-05, AC-06, RE-05
FixCustom dimension b2b_segment extracted in GTM; map all 12 codes including FED.
07

Two lead forms, no shared schema high Studio + Safety

The Studio's inquiry form and School Safety's "Connect with an Expert" form are the same conversion pattern on two properties. Neither emits an event today, and if they're specced separately we'll get two incompatible schemas and no way to compare them.

UnblocksAC-10, AC-11, UX-10
FixOne shared spec: form_start, form_abandon, form_submit, plus email_domain_hash as the join key. Agree it before Oct 5.
08

D&B error codes are never evented medium B2B Hub

Verification produces named failures — NO_MATCH, TIMEOUT, DOMAIN_NOT_WHITELISTED — that reach the user as an error screen and reach analytics not at all. The −30% support target has no way to report itself.

UnblocksUX-04, UX-05
Fixverification_error with error_code and verification_path, alongside the P0 error-copy work.
09

Logged-in accounts have no analytics identity medium B2B Hub

Nothing distinguishes an approved B2B account from an anonymous visitor. The whole reactivation pillar rests on asking "did this account come back," and today that question has no subject.

UnblocksRE-01, RE-03, AW-03
FixGA4 user_id = hashed b2b_account_id, plus an account_status user property. Hash it; don't send the raw id.
10

EDU verification crosses a third-party domain medium EDU Advantage

Students leave for UNiDAYS and come back. Without a referral exclusion the return is counted as new traffic from a referral source, and the verification funnel breaks in the middle.

UnblocksUX-09, AC-08
FixReferral exclusion for the UNiDAYS domain, plus a verification_return event on the callback.
11

The Studio's canonical points at a different URL medium The Studio

The CMS sets the canonical to /find/b2b/studio.jsp while the live page is /find/shared/b2bHub/theStudio.jsp. On a page with no analytics, nobody would notice the ranking damage.

UnblocksAW-08
FixConfirm which URL is canonical before the CMS ships SEO field control, and audit the other core pages while you're in there.

Three things to raise before Oct 5
01

No federal grant content in the design strategy

The SEO strategy targets federal grant spending, but the design has no grant module, no funding guidance and no downloadable guide — Alyssa's Law is the only regulatory content present. Grant-intent traffic would land on a page with nothing to convert against.

SuggestedEither add a grant section with a gated guide — a second, higher-intent conversion moment — or drop grant terms from the AW-02 keyword set so we're not scoring against content we never built.
02

Template debris from the student page QA

Several hidden frames belong to the UNiDAYS student flow, and a hidden heading on the live form still reads "Auto- Registration &". Project Details also shows a 0/30 character counter — about four words. For comparison, The Studio's existing inquiry form allows 1,024.

SuggestedPre-launch QA pass. Note the category layer is spelled Survailance in the file — that misspelling will propagate into event names if anyone derives them from Figma.
03

A guided-selling widget exists but is switched off scope

A "Let's find the right product for you" helper — three dropdowns and a submit — is fully designed and hidden at every breakpoint. If it ships it's a second intent-capture moment with its own schema; if it doesn't, it should be cut rather than left dormant.

SuggestedGet a decision before launch. A hidden widget that ships later without instrumentation is exactly how The Studio ended up untagged.
