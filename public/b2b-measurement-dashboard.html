North Star: Active B2B Accounts (trailing 12 months)

Approved B2B accounts with at least one order in the last 12 months. The four pillars are not four dashboards — they are the two halves of that number. Awareness → Experience → Acquisition grows the new half. Reactivation defends the half you already have, which is the half B&H is currently losing quietly.

Read the last two tiles together. The dormant base is growing roughly four times faster than the active base, and the single conversion rate the PRD sets a +15% target against has no baseline because it isn't measured. Those are the two facts this dashboard exists to fix.

04 — Reactivation

Does the Hub earn a second visit? Once a customer has a punchout portal, their shopping need is met elsewhere and the Hub's job changes entirely — it becomes the account layer. The PRD is blunt that no mechanism exists to notify existing customers of anything, and has not for about five years. Every metric here is currently unmeasurable for the same underlying reason: logged-in accounts have no identity in analytics.

Instrumentation gaps

Nine findings, ranked by how many metrics above they unblock. The first five came out of walking the live funnel and reading the Figma, not out of the PRD — they are things you can go verify this afternoon.

01

No sign-up CTA on the hub landing page critical

The home page offers only twelve segment tiles. Sign Up first appears one level down, on the segment page. There is no top-of-funnel action to measure, which is why the PRD's baseline-conversion question has stayed open.

UnblocksAC-01, AC-07, NS-03
FixPRD P0 Hub UX story, plus signup_cta_click carrying cta_location.
02

The sign-up form is a hash-routed SPA critical

Registration lives at /find/shared/b2b/b2bSignUp.jsp/org/HE#/createAcc. Hash changes do not fire page_view in GA4 by default, so no step in the form is observable — you can see arrivals and, separately, approvals, with nothing in between.

UnblocksAC-02, UX-04
FixPush a signup_step event on every hash route change, with step_name and step_index.
03

The Studio hub carries no GA4 tag at all critical

Zero traffic or engagement data exists for the page. Already identified as a quick win independent of the CMS build — it needs no redesign, no ticket queue, and no Phase 1 dependency.

UnblocksAW-07
FixStandalone tagging task. Ship it before the baseline period starts, or the Studio is missing from every comparison you make later.
04

The funnel spans two URL namespaces high

The hub is under /find/shared/b2bHub/ but sign-up is under /find/shared/b2b/. Any path-scoped report silently cuts the funnel in half at exactly the moment that matters.

UnblocksAC-01 – AC-03
FixDefine each property as a GA4 content group, never a path prefix. Do this before anyone builds a report on the path.
05

Federal routes outside the hub entirely high

Eleven segment tiles go to /organizations.jsp/org/{CODE}. Federal Marketplace goes to /find/gsa.jsp — a different template in a different namespace. One of twelve segments, and arguably the highest-value one, is absent from any hub-scoped view.

UnblocksAW-05, AC-06
FixAdd /find/gsa.jsp to the hub content group and tag it segment = FED.
06

Segment identity is buried in the URL path high

Segment is expressed as a path suffix — /org/HE, /org/SLK, /org/CPP — not a query parameter or data-layer value. Without extraction there is no segment dimension, and every segment-level question becomes a manual URL exercise.

UnblocksAW-05, AC-06, RE-05
FixGA4 custom dimension b2b_segment, extracted from the path in GTM; map all 12 codes including FED.
07

D&B error codes are never evented medium

The verification paths produce named failures — NO_MATCH, TIMEOUT, DOMAIN_NOT_WHITELISTED — that reach the user as an error screen and reach analytics not at all. The PRD's −30% support target has no way to report itself.

UnblocksUX-04, UX-05
Fixverification_error with error_code and verification_path. Ships alongside the P0 error-copy work.
08

Logged-in accounts have no analytics identity medium

Nothing distinguishes an approved B2B account from an anonymous visitor in GA4. The entire reactivation pillar rests on being able to ask "did this account come back," and today that question has no subject.

UnblocksRE-01, RE-03, AW-03
FixSet GA4 user_id to a hashed b2b_account_id, plus a user property for account_status. Hash it — do not send the raw id.
09

EDU verification crosses a third-party domain medium

Students leave for UNiDAYS and come back. Without a referral exclusion, the return is counted as new traffic from a referral source and the verification funnel breaks in the middle.

UnblocksUX-09, AC-08
FixReferral exclusion for the UNiDAYS domain, plus a verification_return event on the callback.

Read from the Figma, not from a spec. This page is a different animal from the other two: its conversion moment is a lead form, and unlike the hub it has the rare luxury of being measurable correctly from day one — if the events ship with the page rather than after it.

Three things worth raising with the team before Oct 5
01

No federal grant content in the design strategy

The SEO strategy for this hub targets federal grant spending, but the design has no grant module, no funding guidance and no downloadable guide — Alyssa's Law is the only regulatory content present. Grant-intent search traffic would land on a page with nothing to convert against.

SuggestedEither add a grant section with a gated guide (a second, higher-intent conversion moment), or drop grant terms from the AW-02 keyword set so the metric isn't measuring against content you never built.
02

Template debris from the student page QA

Several hidden frames belong to the UNiDAYS student flow, and a hidden heading on the live form still reads "Auto- Registration &". The Project Details field also shows a 0/30 character counter — 30 characters is about four words, which is almost certainly not the intent.

SuggestedPre-launch QA pass. Also worth noting the category layer is spelled Survailance in the file — that misspelling will propagate into event names if anyone derives them from Figma.
03

A guided-selling widget exists but is switched off scope

A "Let's find the right product for you" helper — three dropdowns and a submit — is fully designed and hidden at every breakpoint. If it ships it is a second intent-capture moment with its own event schema; if it doesn't, it should be cut rather than left dormant in the file.

SuggestedGet a decision before launch. A hidden widget that ships later without instrumentation is how the Studio page ended up untagged.
