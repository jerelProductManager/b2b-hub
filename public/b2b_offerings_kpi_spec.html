How to read this spec Adoption vs. impact · why the adoption column is red · four conversion objects · what is deliberately excluded
How to read this. Adoption and impact only mean anything when they are anchored to something we ship — so each theme opens by naming its surfaces, and every metric under it answers one of two questions about those surfaces. Adoption: is it reaching people and being used? Impact: is the business moving as a result? Baselines are marked TBD because no data pull has happened yet; targets appear only where the PRD already commits to one. Both are placeholders, not commitments.
Why the adoption column is mostly red. Adoption metrics measure surfaces, and on three of the four offerings the surface either carries no tag (The Studio), has not shipped yet (School Safety), or does not exist at all — the B2B Hub home page has no sign-up CTA to be adopted, and there is no proactive-communication mechanism for existing customers to adopt. Impact metrics fare better only because order and account data lives in the warehouse regardless of what the front end does. Read a red adoption row as a product gap first and a tagging gap second.
Four offerings, four different conversion objects. An approved organization account (Hub), a verified individual student (EDU), and two lead forms (Studio, School Safety) are not the same event and must never be summed. The only figure that legitimately rolls up across all four is AC-10 Qualified Action Rate — each property's own conversion over its own qualified sessions.
Deliberately not in this register. Canonical integrity and template accessibility conformance are one-time audits rather than metrics that move, so they sit in the gap list instead. Both still need action: The Studio's canonical currently points at /find/b2b/studio.jsp while the live page is /find/shared/b2bHub/theStudio.jsp, and no template has been checked against WCAG AA — which public-sector buyers do ask about.
